What We Provide

Three capabilities. We execute each one to the same standard whether we handle the entire project or only a portion of it.

01 — Permitting & Land Access

Seismic Permitting & Right-of-Way Acquisition

Over 40 years of successfully permitting projects in 23 states using a proven team approach. An experienced manager provides direct oversight and maintains clear communication with the client throughout.

We secure the agreements and access rights a project needs before survey and exploration work can begin, working land by land and landowner by landowner. Our team of mineral agents, surface agents, GIS mappers and coordinators, and support staff keeps the work moving with comprehensive reporting at every stage.

  • Full landowner payment services — check generation and tracking, delivery, 1099 generation, and required IRS filings
  • Direct coordination with municipal, county, state, and federal agencies
  • Work across private, state, federal, and tribal lands
  • Field coordination once crews are on the ground

02 — Title & Ownership

Title Curative

One of the most critical tasks on any exploration or production project is confirming that the proper chain of mineral ownership has been correctly researched and identified on every tract. A title issue discovered late can stop work entirely.

Our title agents work across the country to research ownership thoroughly and resolve issues before they become delays. The work is coordinated directly with the permitting and access process.

  • Mineral ownership determination for permitting
  • Full title search back to sovereignty when required
  • Land ownership title opinions
  • Mineral rights title opinions
  • Surface title opinions

03 — Mapping & Data

GIS Services

Experienced GIS mappers and coordinators, using current software, build comprehensive databases and maps for every project — surface, mineral, topographic, infrastructure, and ownership detail.

The result is a working set of maps and data that remains current as the project moves from evaluation through field operations and closeout.

  • Project-specific maps combining all relevant layers
  • Databases maintained throughout the life of the job
  • Support from first evaluation through final reporting
