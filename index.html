Seismic Permitting & Right-of-Way Acquisition

Tier 01 — Access & Permits

Quick Overview

  • Permit acquisition across private, state, federal, and tribal lands
  • Full landowner payment services (Check generation, tracking, 1099/IRS filings)
  • Agency coordination (Municipal, County, State DOT, BLM/USFS)
  • Dedicated field coordinators staying on site during exploration

How APS Executes the Work

We deploy experienced surface and mineral agents who understand landowner dynamics. Rather than taking a heavy-handed approach, we negotiate access terms with respect and transparency, securing rights efficiently without burning bridges in local communities.

Our direct project managers handle all administrative load—including secure landowner payment processing and agency filings—so project schedules remain intact from start to finish.

Title Curative

Tier 02 — Chain of Title

Quick Overview

  • Mineral ownership determination specifically tailored for permitting
  • Full mineral title search back to sovereignty
  • Surface title and land ownership title opinions
  • Proactive resolution of title defects prior to field entry

How APS Executes the Work

A single cloud on a title can delay an entire exploration program. Our title specialists work in county courthouses and digital archives across the country to verify exact ownership chains back to original sovereignty.

Because our title team works side-by-side with our field permitting agents, title issues are flagged and corrected immediately, avoiding costly downtime when crews arrive on site.

GIS & Mapping Services

Tier 03 — Spatial Intelligence

Quick Overview

  • Comprehensive project maps combining surface, mineral, & infrastructure layers
  • Real-time database tracking updated continuously during field work
  • Custom spatial reporting delivered in client-requested formats
  • Post-project archive data handoff for long-term operations

How APS Executes the Work

Data clarity is essential for decision-making. Our dedicated GIS mappers build customized spatial databases for every assignment, integrating parcel boundaries, topographic data, surface obstacles, and permit status into clear, usable map layers.

These databases remain live throughout the life of the project, giving managers and clients an accurate, real-time picture of permitting progress and field readiness.
